Four programme pillars

Career Orientation & Future of Work

Children explore a wide range of career paths through interactive sessions, strengths profiling and real-world case studies, building curiosity about what is possible and which skills will matter.

Entrepreneurship Development

Pupils learn to think like problem-solvers, spot needs around them and understand the basics of value creation, teamwork and initiative, with attention to the emerging digital economy.

Sexual Purity

Age-appropriate, values-centred sessions on identity, dignity, boundaries and healthy relationships, and the case for sexual purity, grounded in character and self-respect.

Mental Wellness & Self Awareness

Teachable moments on managing emotions, developing resilience and making positive choices, so children can thrive in school and in life.

Why AfriChild exists

Schools carry enormous responsibility, yet the formal curriculum rarely makes room for career awareness, an entrepreneurial mindset, and values and identity. AfriChild brings these into the school, alongside the academic programme and not in competition with it.

How a school partnership works

  1. 1

    Exploratory meeting

    A 30-minute conversation with school leadership about fit, needs and timeline.

  2. 2

    Partnership agreement

    A simple agreement covering responsibilities, schedule and consent terms.

  3. 3

    Parent communication

    The school informs parents; AfriChild provides a template letter.

  4. 4

    Programme launch

    AfriChild delivers a launch session at the school.

From our founder
