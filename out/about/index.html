About AfriChild

A structured, value-centred child development programme delivered inside partner schools.

What we are

AfriChild is a school-based programme of facilitated workshops. It is not an online school and has no student accounts or online classes. Sessions take place in person at partner schools.

Mission

To invest in the generation of tomorrow by building the right knowledge, values, and mindset in children today, inside the schools where they spend most of their waking hours.

Vision

A continent where every child between Grade 1 and Grade 10 enters adulthood equipped with self-awareness, vocational clarity, entrepreneurial courage, and a strong moral compass.

Our founder

Jane Ochuma, PHRi, is a Professional in Human Resource Management with a background in human capital development and workforce readiness. Shaped by her faith, her experience as a mother and her insight into what the labour market demands, she founded AfriChild to bridge the gap between school learning and real-life readiness.
