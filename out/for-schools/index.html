For Schools

Complement your academic programme without adding to your teachers’ workload.

Benefits

  • Strengthens your pastoral care and shows commitment to holistic child development.
  • Gives teachers briefings to recognise and respond to warning signs.
  • Gives pupils a structured, safe space to explore identity, values and the future.
  • Gives parents a workbook to review at home and open conversations.

How it works

  1. 1. Exploratory meeting. A 30-minute conversation with school leadership about fit, needs and timeline.
  2. 2. Partnership agreement. A simple agreement covering responsibilities, schedule and consent terms.
  3. 3. Parent communication. The school informs parents; AfriChild provides a template letter.
  4. 4. Programme launch. AfriChild delivers a launch session at the school.

Teacher support

Through partner organisations, enrolled schools can access guidance and counselling professionals, therapy and mental health professionals for cases needing sustained support, and teacher briefings. A designated AfriChild liaison works with your counselling team, and referrals are coordinated with school leadership informed.

Frequently asked questions

Will this disrupt our timetable?

Age-banding and scheduling are agreed in advance to keep disruption to academic timetables minimal.

What do we need to provide?

AfriChild handles session planning, facilitation and materials, including workbook production and distribution.

How are parents involved?

The school informs parents, and AfriChild provides a template letter. A parent information session or written brief is also available.

How is feedback gathered?

Surveys are shared with the school for feedback and areas of improvement. Individual insights are handled under full confidentiality protocols.
