Programmes

Four pillars, delivered through structured, facilitated sessions, each followed by a workbook activity. Programmes cover Grade 1 to Grade 10, with age-banding agreed with each school.

Career Orientation & Future of Work

Children explore a wide range of career paths through interactive sessions, strengths profiling and real-world case studies, building curiosity about what is possible and which skills will matter.

Sample activities

  • Interactive career sessions
  • Personality-and-strengths profiling
  • Real-world case studies

Expected learning outcomes

  • Wider awareness of career paths
  • Early understanding of their own strengths and interests

Entrepreneurship Development

Pupils learn to think like problem-solvers, spot needs around them and understand the basics of value creation, teamwork and initiative, with attention to the emerging digital economy.

Sample activities

  • Problem-spotting exercises
  • Team-based value-creation activities

Expected learning outcomes

  • Problem-solving mindset
  • Basic understanding of value creation, teamwork and initiative

Sexual Purity

Age-appropriate, values-centred sessions on identity, dignity, boundaries and healthy relationships, and the case for sexual purity, grounded in character and self-respect.

Sample activities

  • Facilitated, age-banded discussions
  • Workbook reflection activity

Expected learning outcomes

  • Clarity on identity, dignity and boundaries
  • A values framework for handling peer and social-media messages

Mental Wellness & Self Awareness

Teachable moments on managing emotions, developing resilience and making positive choices, so children can thrive in school and in life.

Sample activities

  • Facilitated emotion-management sessions
  • Confidence-building group activities

Expected learning outcomes

  • Greater confidence and resilience
  • Better habits for making positive choices
